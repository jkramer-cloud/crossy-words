How to Play

Learn first. Hop second. Collect everything.

  1. Read the upcoming incomplete sentence at the top.
  2. Move left and right across five columns, then hop forward one row at a time.
  3. Safe rows give you room to travel and prepare.
  4. Grammar crossings have moving word tiles and gaps. Time your hop to land on the correct word. The ground ring and Next Hop readout help you judge where you will land. Cyan marks a word platform, red an empty gap, gold a timing hazard, and green safe ground.
  5. Roads and rivers have moving vehicles and logs. Hop across safely.
  6. Punctuation crossings sometimes use floating word logs.
  7. Arcade difficulty controls vehicle speeds, train frequency, word movement, and timing. Grade selection controls only grammar content.
  8. World badges reward grammar answers, obstacle crossings, and completing a whole region.
  9. Review mistakes after grammar misses or timeouts. Try the mini retry before restarting.
  10. Earn coins for correct hops and combo streaks.
  11. Visit the Capsule Shop to unlock random characters.
Arcade: Incorrect answers, obstacles, or running out of time end the run.
Practice: No timer; grammar mistakes give coaching and let you try again. Practice mode disables fatal traffic hazards.
